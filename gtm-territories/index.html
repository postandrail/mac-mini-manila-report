The Arca Gardener Loop

1. TerritoryOwn one source or motion deeply: jobs, LinkedIn comments, creators, competitor ads, SEO keywords, website visitors.
2. HarvestBuild an insular Claude Code project: scraper, spreadsheet, classifier, normalizer, or dashboard.
3. EnrichAdd company, person, email, role, intent, angle, and evidence fields. Keep source URLs.
4. ActivatePush clean records into approved Arca systems: Instantly, HeyReach, CRM, ad account, CMS, or reporting.
5. LearnReport volume, quality, reply rate, meetings, CPL, CAC, and what the next scrape should change.

Assign These To The Current 4

These are the highest-leverage territories because they can create pipeline quickly while staying inside small, teachable projects.

1Jobs intent scraper

Hiring-intent territory: OnlineJobs.ph, Indeed, LinkedIn jobs

Find companies actively hiring VAs, SDRs, marketers, appointment setters, AI automation roles, or offshore operators. This matches Arca’s buyer moment better than generic outbound.

Gardener owns
  • Scrape job posts daily.
  • Classify role, budget, location, tools, urgency.
  • Enrich company and hiring manager.
  • Create CSV batches with evidence links.
Arca system handoff

Verified leads go to Instantly. High-intent posts can also become manual LinkedIn connects or loom-style audits.

Corpus match: automate a job role and cold email the industrysource
2LinkedIn/HeyReach

LinkedIn engager territory: KOL posts, commenters, likers

This is the cleanest SDR gardener motion: find conversations where buyers reveal pain, scrape engagers, enrich, and activate via HeyReach or cold email.

Gardener owns
  • Maintain a list of 50-100 relevant KOLs.
  • Scrape post commenters and likers.
  • Tag pain, role, company type, and angle.
  • Write first-line personalization.
Arca system handoff

Send to HeyReach for connection/comment workflows or to Instantly after Apollo + MillionVerifier enrichment.
